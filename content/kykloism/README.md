Kykloism is an economic system built on a blockchain. The currency Kyx (also from Kyklos) decays over time and is automatically equally and universally across all participants created as UBV at a rate of 1 Kyx per minute (universal basic value, since 1 Kyx represents one minute of existence). This way Kyx (also from Kyklos) is only a medium of exchange as it loses value and can't be stored. The store of value will be smart contacts (extremely flexible, no prescriptions, maybe laws will develop but Kykloism shouldn't care) that share revenue in endeavors (and can share between each other, so anyone in the supply chain could benefit from the end product sale), so you keep getting rewards long term from things you've contributed to, you just constantly dilute, which you want to avoid so you become efficient. Anyone can create a network and admit new people too their networks. Then networks can federate based on who they trust. You'll only be and to trade within your network. Countries trust each other and their states, states trust their counties, the countries their towns (if we take the US as an example). It's directional. You can also trust all grandchildren and disconnect parts of the larger networks you don't want to trust. You can transfer your identity from one network to another, with consent of the accepting party of course. Contracts are between unique entities so they can transfer networks and owners.
